# AI Lab

AI experiments from my TikTok/Instagram channels. Each folder is one video: the result you can leverage, and the prompt to recreate it from scratch/build on.

Instagram: dudu.experiments

## Projects

| # | Project | What it is | Try it | Prompt |
|---|---------|------------|--------|--------|
| 01 | Travel planning platform | A page where friends coming from different cities choose a one-week holiday together (made for our specific use case, you'll have to adapt the cities and source data to your use case) | https://duduexe.github.io/ai-lab/01_travel_platform/ | [prompt](01_travel_platform/prompt.md) |
| 02 | Who Gets a Seat | A guest list alignment platform where two people agree on who to invite to their wedding, in seven steps ([guide](02_who_gets_a_seat/guide.pdf)) | https://duduexe.github.io/ai-lab/02_who_gets_a_seat/ (preview only, see its README to use it for real) | No prompt needed: attach [index.html](02_who_gets_a_seat/index.html) in Claude ([how](02_who_gets_a_seat/README.md#how-to-use-it)) |

New projects are added as new videos get posted. 

## How to use a project

1. Open the project's live link, or download its `index.html` and open it in your browser.
2. To recreate it yourself, open the project's `prompt.md`, paste it into Claude, and answer the questions it asks.
